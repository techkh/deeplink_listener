## 1.0.0

Handling Custom Deep Links and Universal Links Deep linking allows your app to respond to links, whether they come from emails, websites, or other apps. There are two main types:
- Custom URL Schemes – Works on both iOS and Android, but users are prompted if the app is not installed.
- Universal Links / App Links – Standardized, secure, and seamless links that open the app if installed or fallback to the website if not.
